# Third-party data notices

## Tatoeba sentence pairs

Some Japanese example sentences and their Russian translations are sourced from
[Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/). They are distributed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/) license.

The application uses linked Japanese–Russian sentence pairs from Tatoeba's
official per-language exports. Additional examples marked as curated were
written specifically for this project.

## Vocabulary images

Vocabulary photographs and illustrations sourced from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) and Openverse are bundled
locally with the desktop application. The author, source page and license for
each image are stored in `public/data/word_images.json` and shown alongside the
corresponding dictionary entry. Individual files may use Public Domain, CC0,
CC BY, CC BY-SA or another license recorded in that manifest.

Some abstract Japanese associations were generated specifically for étudier
with OpenAI image generation. Those entries are identified by the
`openai-imagegen` provider in the same manifest.
